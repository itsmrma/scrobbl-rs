# Brag Plan: scrobbl.rs

## What is this app?

A self-hosted, single-owner music listening archive written in Rust, with a React and TypeScript interface.

## The angle

Treat listening history as something worth keeping. Show the real shape of the app through a clearly marked fictional archive, then land on the small but meaningful promise: the archive lives on your server.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

An emerald waveform resolves into the Overview screen. “Your listening history.” A quiet “DEMO ARCHIVE” label makes the fictional data unmistakable.

## Key moments (the middle)

- A Spotify Extended Streaming History ZIP enters the import flow; the persistent report reaches “Complete.”
- A fictional artist page, activity chart, and searchable listening rows show the archive becoming useful.
- The UI pulls back to reveal the lime logo beside “On your server.”

## Outro / punchline

“scrobbl.rs — a listening archive, on your server.” Calm logo landing, no feature promises beyond the current release.

## User flow worth showing

Open the archive → import an extended-history ZIP → browse listening history and activity.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, personal music archive; restrained motion with the product doing the talking
- Interpretation: use the product's dark surfaces and lime accent, with generous holds and a light rhythmic bed.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22.37 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `color-mix(in oklab, #13151b 96%, var(--accent))`, represented as `#14171c`
- Accent: `#c9ee8b` (logo tile uses existing `#caef78`)
- Text: `#f2f3f7`
- Display font: system sans-serif, matching the frontend
- Body font: system sans-serif, matching the frontend
- Strongest visual element: dark application surfaces, lime accent, and the four-bar waveform logo

## Share copy (draft)

scrobbl.rs keeps a searchable music archive on your own server — from Spotify collection and history imports to rankings, artist pages, and activity charts.

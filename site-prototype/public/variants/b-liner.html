A record of every record you've played.

scrobbl.rs is a listening archive you run yourself. It collects what you play, imports what you played before, and keeps all of it on your own server.

scrobbl.rsYour listening history
Sleeve notesWritten for people who still read the back of the record. Everything here is true of the software; the names on the tracks are made up.

Streaming services remember what you played, until they don't. The yearly summary arrives, the list resets, and a decade of listening becomes something you can download once as a ZIP and never open again.

scrobbl.rs opens it. Feed it your Spotify Extended Streaming History or a ListenBrainz export and it rebuilds the past, file by file, without duplicating a single listen if you feed it twice. Connect your own Spotify app and it keeps going, quietly, as long as the server runs.

Then it gives the archive back to you: every artist, album and track with its own page, rankings for any period, the hours and days you listen, the devices you listen on. Rename what's misnamed. Rate what you love. Group the namesakes, and separate them again when you change your mind.

It is one Rust binary with the interface inside it, one SQLite file for your music, and one owner. No account on someone else's server. No telemetry. No reason for it to stop working in ten years.

Side A Collect

A1Now PlayingSpotify

Listens are collected in the background from your own Spotify app, with what's playing right now in the sidebar.

A2The Long ImportZIP · JSON

Extended Streaming History and ListenBrainz JSON, JSONL or ZIP. Interrupted imports resume; repeated imports are idempotent.

A3Thirty Seconds0:30

Plays count in statistics from 30 seconds. Shorter ones remain searchable in history, but don't pad the numbers.

A4Possible Duplicatesreview

When sources disagree, records stay separate until you decide. Anything you remove goes to a trash you can recover from.

A5Unknown, Not Zero—:——

A missing duration stays unknown. Estimated listening time is labelled as an estimate, never passed off as measured.

Side B Keep

B1Rankings for Any Yearcharts

Complete rankings and activity by day, week, month or year, with pages for every artist, album and track.

B2Half a Star½ – 5

Personal ratings in half-star steps, personal artwork, local corrections to names and titles. The originals are kept underneath.

B3Ask the Archiveoptional

Ask in plain words. A local model, off by default, only translates the question; Rust computes every number. Or point it at your own AI server.

B4Collagegrid

Turn any period's top albums or artists into a square collage of covers.

B5Backup & Restore (Verified)JSON · CSV

Exports, portable backups and verified restores. Upgrades make a checked safety copy before touching the schema.

Playing instructions

Best enjoyed on a small server at home, reached over your LAN, a VPN or private HTTPS. Also plays fine on a laptop.

Get it on GitHub →

  1. Put on the container
    # with compose.yaml from the repository
SCROBBL_IMAGE=ghcr.io/itsmrma/scrobbl-rs:main \
docker compose up -d
  2. Or play it from source
    ./scripts/run-local.sh   # → 127.0.0.1:3210
  3. Sign the sleeveCreate the owner account, connect your Spotify app in Settings, and drop in your export.

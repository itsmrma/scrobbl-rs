collect

background · now playing

04

ask

optional · off by default

Ask in plain words. A local model translates the question into a query; Rust runs it and computes every number. The model never sees your rows. Or bring your own OpenAI-compatible server.

? which album did I play most last winter → query top albums · 2025-12-01 … 2026-02-28 · limit 1 Northbound — Ostrava 214 listens 13 h 40 min
05

keep

export · backup · restore

Export JSON or CSV, make portable backups, and verify a restore before you need it. Schema upgrades take a checked safety copy first.

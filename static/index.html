        BRING YOUR MUSIC WITH YOU

        Your history starts here.

        Give your archive a past. Spotify will take care of what comes next.

        01 / GET YOUR DATA

        Ask Spotify for your extended history

        Open your Spotify privacy settings and request Extended streaming history. The standard account export is a different format.

        Spotify privacy settings ↗
        02 / IMPORT YOUR FILES

        Pick your history JSON files

        Unzip the download first. Select one or more music history files, usually named Streaming_History_Audio_*.json. Up to 64 MiB per upload.

        A little care for your data

        Repeat imports are safe. Records already in your archive are skipped.

        Confident matches with Spotify collection become one listen. The imported duration replaces the estimate. Ambiguous matches stay separate.

        Short listens are kept in history. Only imported plays lasting at least 30 seconds count in statistics.

        Only music is imported. Podcast and audiobook records are skipped. Invalid rows are reported.

        Imports commit one file at a time. If a later file fails, earlier files remain saved and you can safely retry the upload.

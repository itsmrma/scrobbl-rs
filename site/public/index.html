Your listening history, kept on your server.

scrobbl.rs collects your Spotify listens, imports years of history, and turns it into a searchable archive with rankings, charts and pages for every artist, album and track. One binary. One owner. Your data.

Install with Docker View source
  • Rust + SQLite
  • Self-hosted
  • No telemetry
  • GPL-3.0-or-later
